Banana Bread With Chocolate Chips and Walnuts

This picture is from Wikipedia (creative commons license) Page name:Banana Bread.


One thing about this banana Bread that you have to know is that it makes a 9 inch Loaf.

I dont really have a quote for this but I have a story. Ever since I learnt that banana bread was from super ripe bananas, I stopped liking it. And if I had a banana bread that was subpar, I would always imagine eating the black bananas. But after trying this bananabread. Which we probably make once a month, I have started to like banana bread again. It never misses I'm telling you."



INGREDIENTS
Ingredient Amount
All purpose Flour 1.5 Cups
Baking soda 1 Teaspoon
Baking powder 1 Teaspoon
Salt 1/4 Teaspoon
Semisweet Chocolate Chips 1/4 Cup
Walnuts, Toasted, Chopped 3/4 Cup
Unsalted Butter, Room Temperature 1/2 Cup, 1 Stick
Sugar 1 Cup
Eggs 2 Large
Mashed, Ripe Bananas 1 Cup
Fresh Lemon Juice 2 Tablespoons
Vanilla Extract 1.5 Teaspoons
  1. Preheat oven to 350 Degrees Farenheit. Butter and flour 9x5x2 1/2 inch metal loaf pan. Whisk first 4 ingredients in medium bowl to blend. Combine chocolate chips and walnuts in small bowl; add 1 tablespoon of flour mixture and toss to coat.
  2. Beat butter in large bowl until fluffy. Gradually add sugar, beating until well blended. Beat in eggs, 1 at a time. Beat in mashed Bananas, Lemon Juice and vanilla extract. Beat in flour mixture. Spoon 1/3 of batter into prepared pan. Sprinkle with half of nut mixture. Spoon 1/3 of batter over. Sprinkle with remaining nut mixture. Cover with remaining batter. Run knife through batter in zigzag pattern.
  3. Bake Bread until tester inserted into center comes out clean, about 1 hour and 5 minutes. Turn out onto rack and cool.
